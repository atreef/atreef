How It Works

Beginning is gentler
than it feels right now.

Reaching out is the hardest part — so I've made the rest simple. Three unhurried steps take you from a first conversation to real, noticeable change.

  1. 01

    Step One

    A real conversation, free

    We start by talking — no forms, no diagnosis, no commitment. You tell me what's been happening; I tell you honestly what I'm hearing and whether I'm the right person to help. Thirty minutes, simply to see if it fits.

  2. 02

    Step Two

    A plan shaped around you

    If it feels right for both of us, we go deeper — together and one‑on‑one. I listen for the pattern underneath the problem and build a plan around your relationship and your goals, never a one‑size‑fits‑all script.

  3. 03

    Step Three

    Change you can actually feel

    Sessions are a full 60 minutes, thoughtfully prepared, and we track where you are against where you want to be. The goal isn't endless weekly visits — it's lasting change, with a clear sense of progress the whole way through.
