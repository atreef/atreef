Practical details

How much does therapy at AtReef Therapy cost?

Couples therapy at AtReef Therapy is $350 per 60-minute session, and individual therapy is $300 per 60-minute session. Couples therapy is private pay. For current fees, insurance details, and out-of-network information, visit the rates page.

Does AtReef Therapy accept insurance?

Insurance may be available for individual therapy through select plans, depending on availability and eligibility. Couples therapy is private pay. For clients with out-of-network benefits, I can provide a superbill for possible reimbursement, but reimbursement depends on the terms of your plan and is not guaranteed.

Do you offer online couples therapy in Massachusetts?

Yes. AtReef Therapy provides online couples therapy to clients who are physically located in Massachusetts at the time of the session. The practice is based in Cambridge, MA, and sessions take place by secure video rather than in person. Both partners must be in Massachusetts during the session.

How long are therapy sessions and how often do we meet?

Therapy sessions are 60 minutes. Many couples begin with weekly sessions so there is enough continuity to understand and change recurring patterns. Frequency can change over time based on your goals, progress, availability, and what is most useful for the work.

How does the free 30-minute consultation work?

The free 30-minute video consultation is a conversation about what brings you to therapy, what you want to change, and whether my approach feels like a good fit. There is no obligation to continue. If we decide to work together, we can then schedule your first therapy session. Learn more about the consultation.

About the work

Who is therapy at AtReef Therapy a good fit for?

AtReef Therapy works with adults and couples across Massachusetts who may be doing well professionally while feeling stuck in anxiety, burnout, perfectionism, recurring conflict, emotional distance, intimacy concerns, betrayal, or major life and relationship decisions. Therapy can also focus on self-understanding, personal growth, and changing patterns that insight alone has not shifted.

What approach do you use for couples therapy?

Couples therapy at AtReef Therapy integrates Gottman Method Couples Therapy with relational, depth-oriented, and evidence-informed approaches. The work is active and structured, with attention to recurring conflict patterns, emotional connection, communication, intimacy, trust, and the deeper dynamics shaping the relationship.

What if therapy has not helped before?

Previous therapy not helping does not necessarily mean therapy cannot be useful now. We look at what helped, what felt missing, what stayed unchanged, and what you need from the work this time. My approach is active and relational, with an emphasis on understanding patterns while also working toward meaningful changes outside the session.

What if my partner is unsure about couples therapy?

Both partners need to be willing to participate, but you do not have to begin couples therapy with the same level of certainty. A consultation can make room for each person's concerns, expectations, and hesitation. If one partner does not want to participate, individual therapy may be a more appropriate place to begin.

How will we know whether therapy is helping?

Therapy should lead to meaningful changes outside the session, not only useful conversations during it. Depending on your goals, progress may look like less escalation, better repair after conflict, clearer boundaries, reduced anxiety, stronger emotional connection, or different decisions under pressure. We review what is changing and adjust the work when something remains stuck.
