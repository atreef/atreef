Meet Dr. Ehsan Adib Shabahang

I am a licensed mental health counselor and the founder of AtReef Therapy, a Cambridge-based psychotherapy practice for couples and adults.

My work is shaped by clinical training, doctoral research in social psychology, and years spent working across cultures in roles where communication, trust, and misunderstanding had real consequences. That background still informs how I listen for what is said, what is avoided, and the patterns underneath both.
