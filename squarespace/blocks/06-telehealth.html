How does online therapy work at AtReef Therapy?

For those who are accustomed to in-person therapy, it can be difficult to imagine that online therapy, delivered through a screen, could be just as effective. However, research suggests that therapy delivered by video can be as effective as in-person treatment for a range of mental health concerns.

AtReef Therapy, PLLC is an online therapy practice based in Cambridge with sessions that are conducted through secure video, allowing therapy to fit into real life without the commute or waiting room.

What is therapy?

Secure video sessions

Therapy at AtReef is online only. Sessions are held through secure video and are not recorded.

Private and discreet

Meet without a waiting room, commute, or public office visit. AtReef Therapy provides secure video sessions, offering a more private and discreet way to access therapy from anywhere in Massachusetts.

Scheduling flexibility

Scheduling is designed to work around demanding professional and personal lives, with secure video sessions and flexible appointment options. Therapy should fit into your week, not become another source of stress.
